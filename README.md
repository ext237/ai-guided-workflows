# AI-Guided Workflows

A collection of step-by-step AI-guided tutorials and interactive helpers, written in Markdown for use with ChatGPT and other AI assistants.

## What Is This?

These workflows turn AI assistants into interactive guides for real-world tasks, including software configuration, troubleshooting, optimization, and more.

Rather than presenting a lengthy list of instructions, each workflow guides users through a process one step at a time, adapting to their needs and experience level.

## How to Use

1. Browse the repository and select a workflow.

2. Copy the workflow's Raw Markdown URL.

3. Open your preferred AI assistant and enter:

   > Read the instructions at [URL] and guide me through the process step by step. Ask one question at a time and wait for my response before continuing.

4. Follow the instructions provided by your AI assistant.

**Note:** Your AI assistant must support reading external URLs. Otherwise, copy the Markdown contents directly into your conversation.
